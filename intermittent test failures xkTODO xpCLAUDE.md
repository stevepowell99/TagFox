# Intermittent test failures

Faults that fail a run of the full suite now and then and pass when the test is rerun alone. The record is the `runner` session's full suites in `C:\dev\TagFox-runner` on 25 and 26 October 2026: 20 suites of 39 to 41 runs each.

- **`a test Electron outliving its test ends itself` failed "with its parent gone, it ends by itself within 8 seconds"** once (25 October 2026, evening).
- **A test TagFox now and then acts on no key at all, with under 1GB of RAM free.** Both tests now end each failed check with what became of its keys, from the page's own record of them: "never reached the page", "reached the page at …, no entry matched" (or "taken before the key table"), or "entry ran (…)". The next failure says which.
  - `option, dialog and folder keys` (`runner-key-actions.cjs`): from its first set-up step every keypress left the page unchanged (26 October 2026, with about 1GB free).
  - `runner stars open in order` (`runner-stars.cjs`): Ctrl+Shift+1 and Ctrl+Shift+2 each left the folder unchanged for the full 8 seconds, though both stars were listed with their numbers just before (26 October 2026, 20:21 UTC, 0.8GB free, in a suite on `b65d2d3`).
  - Each passed alone afterwards, `runner-stars` three times out of three.
- **`option, dialog and folder keys` has lost its test TagFox mid-run twice** ("CDP connection closed: the test TagFox went away"), at 03:25 and 20:41 UTC on 26 October 2026, each time as the laptop woke from sleep (Kernel-Power events 506 and 507 in the System log). The second came at "Enter on a button", 282 seconds into a run that took 32 when repeated: the laptop had gone into Modern Standby on its idle timeout at 20:36:45 and woke to a keypress at 20:40:47. The first came at a moment's wake in the middle of a night's sleep, which began at 22:48. On battery the laptop sleeps after 6 minutes idle; closing the lid sleeps it at once. What closes the connection on waking is not known: the harness now prints a test Electron's exit code, signal and stderr tail when it ends before the test closes it, so the next loss says whether the TagFox crashed or quit. Windows logs bursts of "LiveKernelEvent 1a8" and "1b8" reports (its black-screen diagnostic dumps) about 20 times a day, at many wakes and at other times too, with no screen going blank; they coincided with both losses and are not evidence of anything here.
- **`viewer in runner` fails "and closes the box" after a Quick TODO save**: twice on 25 October 2026, passing alone each time.
- **`runner status line` failed "with no message it shows a tip"** once (25 October 2026, evening).
- **Two checks fail while Everything is slow.** With about 1GB of RAM free on 24 October 2026, Everything's index was paged out and a search that finds nothing took 1.7s. `refresh visibility` then read "60 hits" where the "Refreshed …" report should be, so a later search (probably Smart's narrow or probe) writes its hits line over the report; `runner tags` failed "removing it with the chip's x renames it back to beta.txt" and "adding LATER renames both files on disk". Both passed alone once the machine recovered, and neither has failed in a suite since.
- **`markdown preview never writes` reads the disk 200ms after closing the editor**, and failed "closing the editor saves the edit" once (24 October 2026). It has not failed since.
- **Run from a checkout under `.claude\worktrees\`**, `runner layout`, `runner keys`, `runner syntax` and `hover preview` fail on unchanged code and pass from `C:\dev\TagFox`. Runner hides paths with a `.` segment, which probably hides the tests' own files there. TagFox's own background jobs run in `C:\dev\TagFox` (`worktree.bgIsolation: none`), so only a worktree made by hand meets this. Either the tests should not depend on the checkout's path, or such worktrees should sit elsewhere.
