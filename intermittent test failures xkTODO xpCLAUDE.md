# Intermittent test failures

Faults that fail a run of the full suite now and then and pass when the test is rerun alone. The record is the `runner` session's full suites in `C:\dev\TagFox-runner` on 25 and 26 October 2026: 20 suites of 39 to 41 runs each.

- **`smoke` and `load-more regression` both leave their test Electron running ("LEFT RUNNING"), as a pair.** Each test passes and then `run-all.cjs` finds its Electron still there. The pair came together in two suites: stage twenty-four's (25 October 2026, evening) and the one that tested the gmist start guard's new waits (26 October 2026, afternoon, while another session ran layout tests in its own checkout). Neither test has left one alone, and neither did so in the other 18 suites.
- **Other tests leave a test Electron running now and then.** `run-all.cjs` looks again for about 9 seconds before calling a process left running, and a test Electron exits by itself once its test process has gone (`TAGFOX_TEST_PARENT_PID`, `test/orphan-exits.cjs`). Even so, `restart stays in the terminal`, `runner stars open in order` and `fuzz seed 999` each left one on the evening of 25 October, `a test Electron outliving its test ends itself` left one twice that evening (and its check "with its parent gone, it ends by itself within 8 seconds" failed once), and `fuzz seed 7` left one on 26 October. A test Electron spawned by node dies with its parent through libuv's job object, and `app.relaunch` makes a process outside that job, but none of the three on 26 October (`fuzz seed 7` and the pair above) came just after a `restart` line in `tagfox-mainperf-test.log`, so a relaunch does not account for them.
- **`option, dialog and folder keys` (`runner-key-actions.cjs`) once received no key at all**: from its first set-up step every keypress left the page unchanged (26 October 2026, with about 1GB of RAM free). It had lost its test TagFox mid-run once before ("CDP connection closed", 03:25 on 26 October). It passed alone after each.
- **`viewer in runner` fails "and closes the box" after a Quick TODO save**: twice on 25 October 2026, passing alone each time.
- **`runner status line` failed "with no message it shows a tip"** once (25 October 2026, evening).
- **Two checks fail while Everything is slow.** With about 1GB of RAM free on 24 October 2026, Everything's index was paged out and a search that finds nothing took 1.7s. `refresh visibility` then read "60 hits" where the "Refreshed …" report should be, so a later search (probably Smart's narrow or probe) writes its hits line over the report; `runner tags` failed "removing it with the chip's x renames it back to beta.txt" and "adding LATER renames both files on disk". Both passed alone once the machine recovered, and neither has failed in a suite since.
- **`markdown preview never writes` reads the disk 200ms after closing the editor**, and failed "closing the editor saves the edit" once (24 October 2026). It has not failed since.
- **Run from a checkout under `.claude\worktrees\`**, `runner layout`, `runner keys`, `runner syntax` and `hover preview` fail on unchanged code and pass from `C:\dev\TagFox`. Runner hides paths with a `.` segment, which probably hides the tests' own files there. TagFox's own background jobs run in `C:\dev\TagFox` (`worktree.bgIsolation: none`), so only a worktree made by hand meets this. Either the tests should not depend on the checkout's path, or such worktrees should sit elsewhere.
